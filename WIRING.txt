================================================================================
SIDETONE / HEADPHONE OUTPUT (PWM on GPIO 12, Pin 16)
================================================================================

No amplifier needed — headphones need only microwatts.

  GPIO12 ──[ 100 Ω ]──┬──[ 10 µF ]──[ 220 Ω ]──► 3.5 mm jack  Tip + Ring
                      │                (+ to GPIO side)
                   [ 100 nF ]
                      │
                     GND ────────────────────────► 3.5 mm jack  Sleeve

    100 Ω / 100 nF   : RC low-pass, fc ≈ 16 kHz, removes the 244 kHz PWM carrier
    10 µF            : DC blocking (output idles at 1.65 V)
    220 Ω            : level + short-circuit protection (≈ 0.7 mW into 32 Ω,
                       already very loud — run the firmware volume at 10–30 %)
    Tip + Ring joined: mono on both ears

    Firmware: keyer tone <300..1200>   keyer vol <0..100>   keyer test 1|0
    The tone follows the on-device keyer and the TUNE carrier.

================================================================================
MICROPHONE (MAX4466 electret module, ADC0 on GPIO 26 / Pin 31)
================================================================================

    MAX4466 VCC ──► 3V3(OUT)  (Pin 36)
    MAX4466 GND ──► GND
    MAX4466 OUT ──► GPIO 26   (Pin 31)   ADC0

    Output idles at VCC/2 ≈ 1.65 V; the firmware removes the DC. Sampled
    at exactly 8 kHz by the ADC itself (48 MHz / 6000) and moved by DMA.
    Set the module's trim pot to roughly mid-travel, then adjust with
    "mic gain <1..50>" and "mic gate <0..0.5>". Select with "src mic",
    or via the OLED MODE item (USB PC -> USB MIC -> CW).

    Without a microphone connected leave src=pc: an open ADC input is noise.

================================================================================
CW KEY / PADDLE JACK (3.5 mm stereo)
================================================================================

    Tip    ──► GPIO 9   (Pin 12)   dit
    Ring   ──► GPIO 11  (Pin 15)   dah
    Sleeve ──► GND

    Internal pull-ups, active LOW. A straight key on Tip works in
    "keyer mode straight" (either contact keys).
